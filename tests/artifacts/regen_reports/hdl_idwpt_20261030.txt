RAM inference audit:
The initial K4 implementation mapped a working bank and small stores to
registers: 34,924 LUT4, 7,583 FF, four DP16KD. Moving scratch/output writes
out of reset logic produced 19,291 LUT4, 4,447 FF, 84 LUTRAM, four DP16KD.
Gating working-bank reads to actual read states removes discarded cross-port
read/write collision behavior: final 1,439 LUT4, 223 FF, 84 LUTRAM, eight
DP16KD. Final arithmetic/handshake/reset simulation passes after these edits.
No arithmetic, geometry or cycle budget was changed to obtain RAM inference.

Remaining gates:
- Integrated placement/routing and qualified 100 MHz timing; the standalone
  K4 run fails as preserved below.
- Mapped-netlist equivalence (Yosys supplied DP16KD model is a black box).
- Board integration, DMA/prefetch and bounded stalls.
- Aggregate coefficient BRAM including voices, fades and staging.
- Actual carrier/operator/mix/effects and tier admission costs.

changes those resources nor claims complete hardware conformance.
Python regression scope remains the separately recorded lifting report;
the full Python suite was not repeated for this standalone HDL addition.
