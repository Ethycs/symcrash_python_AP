Unresolved hardware costs of the candidate:
  At H32/K4, interpolating both sine/cosine from four anchors adds
  32*64*4*2*4 = 65,536 coefficient-by-weight products per block.
  It performs 65,536 int16 source reads (131,072 logical bytes/block), before
  any cache/reuse optimization. This is not measured external DMA traffic.
  Weight construction uses up to 32*4*3 = 384 variable-denominator divisions
  per newly selected pitch, plus fixed-power-of-two product reductions.
  The new signed Q30 interpolation weights occupy 32 bits. These are new
  interpolation intermediates, not changes to int16 stored/audio coefficients.
  Their cost on 18x18 DSP blocks still needs explicit mapping and scheduling.
  Python/JIT speed does not establish that mapping or its cycle count.

The total hardware deadline remains 133,333 cycles/block at 100 MHz, including
admitted voices, transfers, control, tails, effects and mixing. Neither the
existing operator-chain tick model nor these single-voice host measurements
prove that complete bound. Shared memory, two-block prefetch, working buffers,
voice tiers, and actual FPGA timing remain required. The compact candidate
must not be accepted solely because its file fits in the coefficient partition.
